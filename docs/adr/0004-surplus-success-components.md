# Success Components reward excess required output

Replace authored fixed success grants with one independently random typed Component per unused point in listed capabilities after paying one complete satisfied requirement arm, including Direct-route burns and final Re-entry. Per the user's OR clarification, output toward either arm counts—including partially fulfilled alternatives—while shared capabilities count once and unrelated outputs remain excluded; choose the satisfied arm leaving the most unused output. POWER 2 + DETECTION 2 against POWER 3 OR DETECTION 2 therefore earns two Components, superseding the original restriction to surplus within the satisfied arm alone. Failed checks and responses with no unused listed output earn nothing; other success benefits and Sacrifice/Mission/cannibalisation income remain separate, and zero rewards consume no randomness.
